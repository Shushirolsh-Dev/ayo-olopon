An homage to Yoruba Ọ̀pọ̀ tradition. Rule variations exist across families and regions.

How to play

  • Board starts with 4 seeds in each of 12 pits.
  • Choose a pit on your side; sow seeds forward, one in each pit, skipping your emptied pit when passing it.
  • Capture when your final seed lands in an opponent pit with 2 or 3 seeds. Capture backward through qualifying pits.
  • If your opponent has no seeds, feed them if you can. Don't starve them deliberately.
  • When no legal moves remain, remaining seeds are swept to their owners. Highest capture count wins.
